Personal Brand Methodology

Your brand is a feeling. You already have one. This is where you put it into words.

You are not inventing a persona here, and you are not building something that isn't already there. Six elements, used as a lens on yourself — so the thing you already are stops being a feeling you can't explain and becomes something you can say out loud.

01

First, see it in people you already know.

Nobody here handed us a playbook and none of this is a formula. These are eight people whose brands are unmistakable, read through the same six elements you are about to turn on yourself — so you can see what each element looks like once someone has actually articulated it. Take them as inspiration, not instruction.

02

Now it's your turn.

None of this is invented. The six elements are already true about you — the work is getting them out of your head and into language you can actually use. Eighteen questions, one honest session. Answer them properly and you leave with your own words, written down.
